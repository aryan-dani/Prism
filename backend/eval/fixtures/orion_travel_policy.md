# Orion Tools Pvt Ltd — Travel & Reimbursement Policy (2026)
# Demo fixture for session-scoped uploads. Not a Kohler document.

## 1. Per diem
Domestic per diem is INR 3,250 per day for metro cities and INR 2,100 for non-metro cities.
Per diem is paid without receipts. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 2. Approval bands
Claims up to INR 8,000 are approved by the reporting manager.
Claims from INR 8,001 to INR 60,000 need the department head.
Claims above INR 60,000 need the VP Finance and a pre-approval memo.

## 3. Submission deadline
All travel claims must be submitted within 21 calendar days of return.

## 4. Hotel caps
Metro hotel cap is INR 9,500 per night; non-metro is INR 6,000 per night.
