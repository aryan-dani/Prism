# MERIDIAN FIXTURES INC. — FINANCE POLICY MANUAL

**Document owner:** Finance Department
**Applies to:** All employees submitting expense claims, purchase requests, or invoices on behalf of Meridian Fixtures Inc.
**Currency:** All amounts in Indian Rupees (₹) unless stated otherwise.
**Version:** 1.0 (Synthetic — Prism Project)

## 1. Objectives

This manual establishes clear, auditable rules for expense reimbursement, invoice processing, approval authority, per diem rates, and budget administration, ensuring consistent financial control across all Company locations.

## 2. Expense Reimbursement Limits and Approval Hierarchy

### 2.1 Approval Thresholds by Claim Value
Every expense claim is routed for sign-off based on its total value, in addition to the employee's Reporting Manager approval required by HR Policy Section 3.2.

**Exact bands (inclusive of the stated lower and upper bounds):**

- Claims of **₹0 to ₹5,000** (inclusive): Self-certified by claimant; no additional Finance sign-off required.
- Claims of **₹5,001 to ₹25,000** (inclusive): Reporting Manager sign-off required. Example: a ₹15,000 claim falls in this band and needs Reporting Manager sign-off.
- Claims of **₹25,001 to ₹1,00,000** (inclusive): Department Head sign-off required (in addition to Reporting Manager). Example: a ₹40,000 claim falls in this band.
- Claims **above ₹1,00,000**: Chief Financial Officer (CFO) sign-off required (in addition to Department Head).

| Claim Value | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹5,000 | Self-certified by claimant; no additional Finance sign-off required |
| ₹5,001 – ₹25,000 | Reporting Manager sign-off required |
| ₹25,001 – ₹1,00,000 | Department Head sign-off required (in addition to Reporting Manager) |
| Above ₹1,00,000 | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) sign-off required (in addition to Department Head) |

These thresholds apply to the **total value of a single claim**, not to individual line items within it. Splitting a single expense into multiple smaller claims to avoid the correct approval level is treated as a Code of Conduct violation (HR Policy Section 4.2) and may trigger disciplinary review.

### 2.2 No-Receipt Threshold
Original itemized receipts are mandatory for any expense line item above **₹500**. Line items of ₹500 or below may be claimed with a self-declaration if a receipt was not issued (e.g., minor local conveyance), up to a maximum of **₹1,500 in unreceipted claims per month** per employee.

### 2.3 Reimbursement Timelines
- Claims are reviewed by the Reporting Manager within **5 working days** (per HR Policy Section 3.2).
- Finance completes its sign-off and processes payment within **10 working days** of receiving a fully-approved claim.
- Claims submitted more than **30 calendar days** after the expense date require a written justification and Department Head approval before Finance will process them at all.

## 3. Per Diem Rates (Domestic and International Travel)

### 3.1 Domestic Per Diem
Per diem is a flat daily allowance covering meals and incidentals **not otherwise claimed** as itemized expenses, paid in addition to hotel/travel costs:

| City Tier | Per Diem (per day) |
|---|---|
| Tier-1 (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune) | ₹2,000 |
| Tier-2 (state capitals and other major cities) | ₹1,500 |
| Tier-3 (all other locations) | ₹1,200 |

### 3.2 International Per Diem
| Region | Per Diem (per day, USD) |
|---|---|
| North America / Western Europe | $110 |
| Rest of World | $80 |

International per diem is paid in INR at the prevailing Company-notified exchange rate on the date of travel approval, not the date of travel.

### 3.3 Partial-Day Travel
Travel days of less than 12 hours are paid at **50% of the applicable per diem rate**. Travel days of 12 hours or more are paid at the full rate.

## 4. Invoice Submission and Processing (Vendor Invoices)

### 4.1 Submission Window
Vendors must submit invoices within **30 calendar days** of delivery of goods/services. Invoices submitted after this window require written justification and Department Head approval before processing.

### 4.2 Purchase Order (PO) Requirement
A Purchase Order is **mandatory** for any procurement above **₹50,000** before goods/services are ordered. Invoices above ₹50,000 received without a corresponding pre-approved PO are held and escalated to the Department Head for retrospective justification — this is treated as a process exception, not routine practice.

### 4.3 Invoice Approval Hierarchy
Vendor invoices follow the **same approval thresholds as Section 2.1** (₹5,000 / ₹25,000 / ₹1,00,000 bands), applied to the invoice total, with the additional requirement that any invoice above ₹50,000 must reference a valid PO number (Section 4.2).

### 4.4 Payment Terms
Standard payment terms are **Net 45** from the invoice approval date, unless a different term is specified in the vendor's master agreement. Early payment discounts negotiated below Net 15 require CFO approval.

### 4.5 Three-Way Match
Accounts Payable performs a three-way match (Purchase Order, Goods Receipt Note, Invoice) before releasing payment for any invoice above ₹25,000. Discrepancies beyond **2%** of invoice value are held for Department Head review before payment release.

## 5. Budget Allocation and Administration

### 5.1 Annual Budget Cycle
The annual operating budget is prepared during **January–February** for the fiscal year beginning **April 1**, following the same fiscal-year convention as HR Policy leave-year accrual.

### 5.2 Departmental Budget Authority
Each Department Head has spending authority up to their approved annual departmental budget. Any single spending decision within budget but exceeding **₹1,00,000** still requires CFO sign-off per Section 2.1, regardless of budget availability — budget approval and transaction-level approval are separate controls.

### 5.3 Budget Variance Review
Departments exceeding **10% variance** against quarterly budget (either over or under) must submit a variance explanation to Finance within **10 working days** of quarter-close. Variance beyond **20%** requires a revised forecast to be presented to the CFO.

### 5.4 Contingency and Reallocation
A Company-wide contingency reserve of **5% of the total annual operating budget** is held centrally and released only with CFO approval, for expenses not anticipated in departmental budgets. Departments may not reallocate more than **15% of their own approved budget** between line items without Finance approval.

### 5.5 Capital Expenditure (CapEx)
Any single capital purchase above **₹5,00,000** (equipment, tooling, facility improvement) requires a CapEx justification form and CFO approval, regardless of whether it was pre-approved in the annual budget. CapEx items are capitalized and depreciated per the Company's fixed-asset policy (straight-line, useful life per asset class) rather than expensed in the year of purchase.

## 8. Record Retention

All expense claims, invoices, and approval records are retained for a minimum of **7 years** from the date of transaction, in line with standard statutory audit requirements, and must be produced on request during internal or external audit.
